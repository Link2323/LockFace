LockFace Face Recognition Privacy Notice and Informed Consent

Version: 1.1
Effective date: October 26, 2026
Applicable software: LockFace 1.9.0 and later (including the installer, LockFaceService, LockFaceCredentialProvider, LockFaceConsole and related components)

Important: Face information used to identify a person is sensitive personal information or special-category personal data under applicable law. Read and acknowledge this notice before enrollment. Installation requires acknowledgment of reading; processing your own face requires separate consent during enrollment. Article 6 describes the scope of consent; Article 7 describes deletion and withdrawal.

Article 1. Software and parties responsible for processing

1.1 “Software” means the face recognition sign-in software and its components released by the LockFace open-source project.
1.2 “Project” or “we” means the maintainers of the LockFace open-source project, available at https://github.com/Link2323/LockFace.
1.3 “You” means the individual who installs or uses the Software.
1.4 “Face information” means facial biometric information, recorded electronically or otherwise, relating to an identified or identifiable individual. Face feature vectors extracted from camera images and saved by the Software are called “face templates”.
1.5 “Local processing” means processing performed on the computer running the Software, without transferring the data to the Project or any third party.
1.6 We provide locally running face sign-in software. With your authorization, it captures face images locally, extracts and saves templates, then reads and compares templates and uses the corresponding sign-in credentials during authentication. We do not provide a cloud face recognition service. The Software does not send this face information back to Project servers or give us network access to the local face database. Article 8.2 covers feedback that you voluntarily submit.
1.7 Responsibility for local data depends on the actual use. For personal use, you manage the device and local enrollments. When a business, school or other organization deploys the Software for others, the party that actually determines the purposes and means of processing must fulfill the corresponding management duties. Rules about processors, controllers and personal or household exemptions depend on applicable law and the specific use. Article 10 covers organizational deployment.

Article 2. Purposes and necessity

2.1 The Software processes face information for the optional face sign-in feature you choose. Enrollment generates or updates local templates. At unlock, face features from camera images are compared against templates enrolled for accounts on this computer. The best match, conditions distinguishing accounts and liveness results determine the account. After successful verification, the corresponding sign-in credentials are submitted to Windows.
2.2 Face sign-in is optional and is not the only authentication method. Templates identify a person, account identifiers associate templates with accounts, and the saved encrypted password completes Windows sign-in after successful recognition. You can choose not to enroll a face and continue using a password or another sign-in method supported by the system.
2.3 The Software does not use face information for advertising, profiling, credit assessment, algorithm training or other purposes unrelated to sign-in verification.
2.4 Template learning is designed to make small updates to existing local templates using face features from a successful sign-in. It does not change the built-in recognition model and is not model training. The current version disables this feature and does not automatically update templates through it. Before enabling it in the future, we will update the relevant explanations and obtain separate valid authorization where required by applicable law.

Article 3. Information processed and camera activation

3.1 The Software processes the following information locally:
(1) Camera images: obtained during face authentication, enrollment preview and capture, and used in memory as required for real-time preview, face detection, alignment, liveness detection and feature extraction. Preview images appear in the enrollment interface. Frames are cached briefly in memory and are not written to disk.
(2) Face feature vectors: numerical features computed by the model, currently 128 floating-point values, used for comparison with local face templates.
(3) Liveness detection results and scores: used to assess whether the images come from a real person.
(4) Account identifiers: Windows account name, UPN and SID, used to associate templates with accounts.
(5) Encrypted Windows password: saved locally to complete sign-in after recognition succeeds, never stored as plaintext. Article 5.1 describes encryption.
(6) Operating logs: diagnostic information that may include times, components, account identifiers, recognition distances, thresholds and error codes. Logs contain no face images and do not record plaintext passwords.
(7) Local settings and authorization records: software configuration, display preferences, camera exposure adjustment state, notice version, acknowledgment or consent time, and pending withdrawal status. These support operation, remembering choices and completing withdrawal. They contain no face templates but may still be personal information when linked to an account.

3.2 The Software does not save face photos, videos or raw camera frames. It does not collect fingerprints, identity documents, location, contacts, messages or other personal information unrelated to sign-in verification.
3.3 Face detection, feature extraction, liveness detection and matching all take place on this computer. The camera is activated only during face authentication or enrollment preview and capture.
3.4 Once you have consented to the current privacy notice and no withdrawal is pending, the enrollment interface may start a preview automatically, before you click “Start capture”. A valid existing consent record allows preview to resume when you reopen enrollment. During pending withdrawal, preview does not resume, renewed consent and new enrollment saves are blocked. See Article 7.

Article 4. Storage location and retention

4.1 Face templates, account identifiers and encrypted passwords are stored in data\users.dat under the locally configured data directory. The default path is C:\Program Files\LockFace\data\users.dat. Local database backups and temporary write files may also be created.
4.2 To continue providing face sign-in, the Software retains enrolled templates, account identifiers and encrypted passwords until the last face is deleted, the account is cleared, withdrawal completes or uninstallation cleans them up. Retention is limited to the period needed for the feature. The current Software does not automatically delete data after inactivity or detect events such as an employee leaving or a device changing hands. Complete the appropriate cleanup when you stop using it or the processing purpose ends.
4.3 Camera images, unsaved samples and inference intermediate results are held temporarily in memory during preview, capture and authentication. Related caches are released when the relevant flow ends, preview stops, consent is withdrawn or the program exits. They are not written to photo or video files.
4.4 Operating logs use a 14-day window when checking active logs and rotating old files. Archives beyond that window are removed during rotation or conversion of older log encodings. The 14 days are a rotation-check window: actual cleanup depends on components running and writing logs. Inactivity, files in use or permission problems can cause some logs to remain longer.
4.5 Database backups and temporary files are cleaned as described in Article 7.3. Additional backups do not extend the permitted use of deleted information. Local settings support continued use, personal consent records support version checks, and withdrawal markers support deletion retries. Articles 6.4 and 7.6 describe where authorization records are kept and how they are cleaned up.
4.6 Rely on the execution result displayed by the Software to determine whether deletion or withdrawal has completed. Where a deployment legally requires shorter or fixed retention, automatic destruction or strict log retention, the deploying party must arrange the necessary management and technical measures and confirm cleanup.

Article 5. Security measures and recognition risks

5.1 The SYSTEM service encrypts passwords with Windows DPAPI in user scope, binding them to the service identity and this computer. An ordinary user who obtains only a current-format encrypted password file cannot directly decrypt it under their own identity.
5.2 Face feature vectors and account identifiers are protected by local file permissions. In a production installation, the data directory grants access only to SYSTEM and Administrators. These fields are not separately encrypted and can be read by parties with the necessary access permissions.
5.3 The Software also checks model integrity and avoids logging plaintext passwords. Password encryption and template access controls operate separately as described in Articles 5.1 and 5.2. Operating logs retain account identifiers and other diagnostic information described in Article 3.1; they are not treated as anonymized data.
5.4 Liveness detection can reduce the success rate of attacks using photos, videos, screen replays or masks. It cannot fully prevent attacks using high-quality masks, 3D printing, deepfakes, camera injection or compromised drivers or operating systems.
5.5 Lighting, obstruction, distance, angle, aging, makeup, twins or similar-looking individuals may cause recognition failures (false rejections) or incorrect acceptance (false matches).
5.6 An attacker with elevated administrator or SYSTEM access, or offline access to the system drive, may still decrypt or steal relevant data. Malware, physical access by others, compromised account privileges or coercion may also lead to data disclosure or unauthorized sign-in.
5.7 The Software does not promise 100% recognition accuracy or security and cannot guarantee absolute safety. Face sign-in is a convenience feature and should not be relied upon as protection in high-security situations, including storage of highly sensitive data, shared computers or unattended environments. If recognition fails, sign in with your password.

Article 6. Your choices and authorization for face processing

6.1 You choose whether to install the Software, enroll your face or use face sign-in. Refusing or withdrawing authorization for face processing does not affect Windows password sign-in. You can also install the Software without enrolling a face.
6.2 Installation acknowledgment and authorization for face processing are separate. The checkbox in the installer acknowledges reading this notice. Before face enrollment is enabled, the person whose face will be processed must separately authorize it in the enrollment interface. An administrator's or installer's acknowledgment does not authorize processing on behalf of another person. Applicable law governs the required form of authorization.
6.3 Authorization in the enrollment interface covers only the following:
(1) I understand that face information is sensitive personal information and that recognition involves risks of false matches, false rejections and attacks.
(2) I expressly consent to camera capture of my face on this computer, extraction and storage of templates, and use of those templates together with account identifiers and an encrypted password for my face sign-in verification. Article 4 describes retention.
(3) I understand that I can refuse or withdraw consent and continue using password sign-in. Article 7 describes deletion, withdrawal and the conditions for completion.
(4) Having understood the processing, retention, security measures and risks in this notice, I voluntarily and expressly give the authorization above, solely for processing my own face for sign-in.

6.4 The installation acknowledgment version and time are stored locally under HKLM\SOFTWARE\LockFace. Each Windows user's face-processing consent version and time are stored separately under HKCU\SOFTWARE\LockFace. Pending withdrawal is recorded separately so deletion can continue after interruption or restart. These records support version checks, recording choices and completing withdrawal; they contain no face templates but may still be personal information when associated with an account. Local version and time records are not authenticated proof of identity or an electronic signature. Any additional legally required authorization formalities must also be met.
6.5 After you consent during first enrollment, the Software remembers your authorization for that Windows user. It covers later preview, capture, template storage and face sign-in verification within Article 6.3, without requiring another checkbox each time you open the Software, enroll or sign in. You can withdraw at any time. Reconfirmation is required if the notice version changes or the consent record is missing or unreadable. Pending withdrawal must first be completed under Article 7. Each Windows user must authorize separately.

Article 7. Access, correction, deletion and withdrawal

7.1 The enrollment wizard provides local face management: viewing the number and names of enrolled records, deleting individual or all faces, and correcting templates through re-enrollment. This notice enables you to understand, choose, limit or refuse the processing. Contact the Project through an Issue for other rights requests.
7.2 Deleting the last face or clearing the current account also deletes that account's saved identity information and encrypted password. Uninstalling stops use of face sign-in and deletes face data, settings and logs managed by the Software. Some files in use are deleted after Windows restarts.
7.3 Deletion, withdrawal, replacement enrollment and account identity or password updates clean old database backups and temporary files from interrupted writes that the Software manages. Later backups reflect the updated database. These operations logically delete files and records; they cannot guarantee that physical media is unrecoverable, or delete copies made separately by you or third parties, system backups or disk snapshots.
7.4 Click the privacy consent status in enrollment and choose “Withdraw consent”. The Software immediately stops the enrollment camera and capture, clears unsaved samples, and asks the local service to delete all faces, identity information and encrypted passwords for the current account and clean old backups. Before requesting service deletion, it persists a pending withdrawal marker in this user's local settings. If that cannot be saved, an error is reported and no service deletion request is sent.
7.5 Withdrawal completes only after the service confirms deletion and the old consent record has been cleared. The pending marker is then removed. Further use requires new consent and enrollment. If the service is unavailable, files are in use or cleanup fails, the pending marker survives restarts and continues blocking enrollment preview, capture, renewed consent and new enrollment saves. “Retry withdrawal” is provided. An unreadable withdrawal status also keeps these features off. Until deletion is confirmed, previous face sign-in may still work. Check the service, permissions and logs, then retry. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of consent-based processing before withdrawal.
7.6 Completed withdrawal clears this user's consent record and pending marker, but does not automatically clear machine-wide installation acknowledgment or existing logs. Uninstallation cleans machine records and records in the current user's and other currently loaded user profiles. Unloaded profiles or failed cleanup may leave records requiring later administrator cleanup.
7.7 With the necessary file access permissions, a local administrator can copy the database described in Article 4.1 to obtain a local data copy containing face templates. The file also contains identifiers and encrypted passwords for accounts enrolled on this computer; it is not a template file separated by individual. The person making a copy must store and manage it. Article 7.3 describes cleanup limits. The wizard has no separate template-export button.
7.8 Data copies, portability, restriction, objection, complaints and judicial remedies are handled within the scope, conditions and procedures of applicable law. The wizard has no separate processing-restriction switch. In organizational deployments, the party actually processing the data must arrange other appropriate procedures, respond within statutory deadlines and explain legally permitted refusals. Because the database stays on this computer, we cannot remotely read or delete its contents for you.

Article 8. Local processing and disclosure

8.1 Face capture, template storage and recognition matching take place locally. The Software does not upload face information or sell, share, disclose or publicly release it to the Project or third parties. It does not transfer face information across borders or include advertising, analytics, tracking or face-information reporting features.
8.2 Before voluntarily submitting logs or screenshots through GitHub Issues or similar channels, remove or obscure personal information such as account names, SIDs and face images. We use information you voluntarily provide only for troubleshooting. Public Issues may be viewed and copied by anyone and retained under the platform's rules. Do not submit passwords, face templates or the complete credential database.
8.3 Organizations manage their own data copying, remote access and backups. Duties relating to recipients, disclosures and cross-border processing depend on those actual activities. The Software's local processing does not cover these additional arrangements.

Article 9. Protection of minors

9.1 Applicable law and the specific use determine the age of majority, capacity to consent, guardian authorization and other protections. Check local requirements before using the Software for minors.
9.2 In China, consent-based processing of face information of children under fourteen requires parental or other guardian consent and dedicated personal-information processing rules. Elsewhere, follow applicable local requirements.
9.3 The current Software has no age verification, guardian identity/authorization verification or dedicated child enrollment flow. Deployment for minors requires the dedicated rules, valid authorization and protections required by applicable law. An installer's or minor's checkbox does not replace these arrangements. If enrollment does not meet the requirements, stop the relevant use and have the legally entitled individual or guardian request deletion from the party actually processing the data. Article 7 describes local deletion.

Businesses, schools and other organizations deploying it for others must, before collection, supplement this notice with the responsible party, lawful basis, specific retention and destruction arrangements and rights procedures appropriate to their use, complete applicable impact assessments and other legal duties, and provide reasonable, convenient alternatives to face authentication. Employment or management relationships must also allow the voluntary choice required by law. These additional notices and management arrangements are the organization's responsibility.
10.2 This notice reflects existing features and common processing. Specific deployments must comply with mandatory local law and biometric rules and provide additional notices where needed. Supplements must reflect actual features and meet mandatory protections. Publication of the Software does not establish that a deployment has completed compliance checks for every region.
10.3 Where relevant Chinese rules apply, deployments must fulfill requirements concerning separate consent, necessity, alternative authentication, the shortest necessary retention, impact assessments and security measures. Article 5 describes password encryption and template access controls; additional measures required for a deployment must still be implemented as required by law.
10.4 Where EU, EEA or UK data protection law applies, face templates used to uniquely identify a person are subject to special-category data rules. Deployers must verify both a general lawful basis and a special-category processing condition, and meet applicable notice, rights, security and impact-assessment duties. Local processing and interface consent describe product behavior; legal requirements remain deployment-specific.
10.5 Some US states and cities have specific biometric rules. For example, where Illinois BIPA applies, it requires prior written notice of collection or storage, specific purposes and duration, written authorization, and a public retention and destruction policy. Destruction is due when the initial purpose is satisfied or three years after the individual's last interaction with the relevant private entity, whichever is earlier. The Software does not automatically record that legally defined last interaction or enforce such expiry. Applicable deployments must arrange destruction by the earlier deadline and verify other local restrictions.
10.6 Before deploying elsewhere, verify local conditions for face sign-in and provide understandable notice and any required regional rules. Regions not listed here have not thereby undergone legal review. If local requirements are unmet, use passwords or another lawful authentication method.
